- Proof level: **read** (source or spec text read), **specified** (behaviour stated precisely enough to test, inputs read), **run** (executed). The only executions are seat/scout API queries and the scout's WebSocket memory table, cited as such.
- Evidence points at the scout or seat row that holds the primary citation, or is given inline. Claims with no evidence are marked HYPOTHESIS.

## Charter objections both seats raised (accepted)

- PR1, PR3 and PR4 are overturned by their own conditions (J-17, J-03, J-01).
- PR2: taking the hostname out of the tunnel exposes the VPS IP, which also serves versatile.sh. There is no DoS budget in the charter (OD-06).
- PR6: full parity (4K, relay-only, extensions, whiteboard) is the largest cost. These items are deferred behind triggers (OD-08).
- F2 after a restart: presence is empty by design. The floor should read "single authority while running".
